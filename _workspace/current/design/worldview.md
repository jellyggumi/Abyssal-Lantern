## Faction identity (the whole lore, on purpose)

Two castle-states share one valley and one grudge nobody remembers starting.

- **Blue — 청군 (player default)**: methodical siege engineers. Banner: cobalt
  on white. Voice: dry understatement ("이웃집에 소금 빌리러 왔습니다").
- **Red — 홍군 (enemy default)**: theatrical, over-committed attackers.
  Banner: crimson on black. Voice: bombast that undercuts itself.

Tone follows the reference genre's lesson (see trend survey): comedic,
low-lore, zero cutscene budget — the war is a neighborly feud escalated to
trebuchet scale. All flavor text stays within this register.

## Consistency rules (G1 audit basis)

1. Faction color is meaning: blue/red tinting is reserved for team ownership.
   No third faction color ships without a director waiver.
2. Every stage is a place in the same valley: Siege Plains (경계 평원),
   Ashen Bastion (홍군 측 요새 전선), Frostbound Gorge (상류 협곡).
   New stages name their spot in the valley.
3. Units are guild professionals, not soldiers-of-lore: Knight(돌격 계약직),
   Archer(원거리 조합원), Bomber(폭발물 면허 보유). New units get a guild
   framing, not a chosen-one framing.
4. No text may contradict the "nobody remembers why" premise — the war has
   no righteous side, which is what keeps both factions playable.

## Visible-without-reading mandate (UX pillar)

Worldview must land through silhouette, color, and motion first; text is
garnish. Any string that a playtest shows is load-bearing for comprehension
is a G1 defect against this section.
